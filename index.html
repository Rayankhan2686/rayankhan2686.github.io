Two-Body Orbital Propagator

Astrodynamics

A from-scratch propagator that integrates the two-body equations of motion with RK45, including J2 oblateness perturbations. It converts between classical orbital elements and Cartesian state vectors, renders 3D trajectories and ground tracks, and validates nodal regression to within 0.4% of the analytical J2 result across ISS, Molniya, and GEO test orbits.

Python, SciPy, RK45, NumPy, Matplotlib

pyFEA — in progress

Structures · FEA

A linear-static finite-element solver written from the ground up to demonstrate the method itself, not call a library. It reads a YAML model deck, builds the 6×6 constitutive matrix, assembles Tet4 elements into a global stiffness system, solves for displacement and stress, and exports .vtu fields for ParaView. Element eigenvalue spectra are verified for correct rigid-body and deformation modes.

Python, NumPy, FEM, YAML, ParaView
